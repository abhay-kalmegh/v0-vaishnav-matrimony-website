About Us

A Sacred Mission to Unite Devotee Hearts

Vaishnav Vivaah was born from a simple yet profound realization: devotees deserve a matrimony platform that truly understands their spiritual values, practices, and the sacredness of marriage in Krishna consciousness.

Our Story

Founded in 2023, Vaishnav Vivaah emerged when our founder, a devoted Krishna bhakta, experienced firsthand the challenge of finding a life partner who shared the same depth of spiritual practice and commitment to dharmic living.

Mainstream matrimony platforms simply could not capture the essence of what makes a Vaishnav marriage sacred - the shared devotion to Shree Krishna, the alignment of daily spiritual practices, and the importance of Sampraday and Guru lineage.

Today, we serve over 50,000 devotee families across 12 countries, with more than 5,000 successful marriages - each one a testimony to the power of spiritual compatibility in creating lasting, loving unions.

Our Values

Built on Dharmic Principles

Devotion First

We believe that a marriage rooted in Krishna consciousness creates the strongest bond. Every feature we build prioritizes spiritual alignment.

Trust & Safety

Every profile is verified by our team. We maintain the highest standards of authenticity and privacy to protect our community.

Family Involvement

We honor traditional family involvement in matchmaking. Parents and guardians can create and manage profiles, reflecting dharmic values.

Cultural Sensitivity

Our platform understands the nuances of Vaishnav traditions, from Sampraday-specific practices to dietary preferences and guru lineage.

Spiritual Compatibility

Beyond basic matching, we evaluate spiritual practices, devotion level, and lifestyle discipline to ensure meaningful connections.

Global Community

Serving devotees across 12+ countries, we connect the worldwide Vaishnav community while respecting regional traditions and customs.
