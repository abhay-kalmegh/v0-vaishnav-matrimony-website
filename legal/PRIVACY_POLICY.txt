PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Vaishnav Vivaah. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

We do not sell your data. Your profile details are only visible to registered and verified members of the Vaishnav Vivaah community based on your privacy settings.

4. DATA SECURITY
We use industry-standard encryption and secure servers (HTTPS) to protect your information.

5. YOUR RIGHTS
You can edit or delete your profile at any time through the app settings.
